# Product Mission

## Problem

Logging food is the part of fitness tracking people quit. Every competing app makes you search a database, pick a match, then fiddle with a portion — a dozen taps for one meal. The friction, not the motivation, is what ends the streak.

## Target Users

People tracking fitness day to day: calories and macros, workouts, sleep, weight, water, cycle. Plus trainers, who work across a roster of clients from the same app.

Mobile-first in the literal sense — the app is used one-handed, standing up, mid-meal.

## Solution

**Speak instead of typing.** "Two eggs and a coffee" logs both items with real nutrition, resolved through a two-tier pipeline: a curated food database first, Gemini for anything unknown. Voice covers every operation, not just food — workouts, sleep, goals, add/edit/delete.

The guarantee that makes it usable: voice **never** dead-ends. If Gemini blocks or fails, the entry is created from the transcript with zero nutrition for the user to correct. The user is never blocked by the AI.

Manual entry — search, barcode, form — stays available as the secondary path.
